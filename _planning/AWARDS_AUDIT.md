## Coverage and limitations

- All **66 titles** received exact-title award searches. Canonical publisher/proceedings/preprint URLs were attempted for every paper, including the 19 records missing URLs in local content.
- **40 publication pages** returned readable text for award-keyword checks. **26** returned errors or browser challenges. Their author, conference and institution search evidence is recorded, but those publisher pages remain verification gaps.
- **Twitter/X is incomplete.** Twelve identity-linked profiles were attempted and returned empty/blocked pages; tentative Tim/Jina handles also failed. Other members had no verified readable profile located. Indexed mentions were useful leads, but reposts and snippets were not treated as proof of an award or a full timeline review.
- Personal sites and CVs can be stale. Examples include Jina's old publication list, Cheng's pre-UW CV, and Xinyi/Rongwu PDFs readable only through search indexing or returning errors.
- “All papers” here means the **66 site publications**, not every paper ever authored by every member. Member-site publication lists were also scanned for award annotations and followed up when they produced a lead.
- No claim of exhaustive internet coverage is made. The review explicitly preserves inaccessible sources, unknown years and conflicting labels.

The [structured coverage file](awards-audit/coverage.json) records each person, each paper, candidate IDs, source URLs, evidence status and limitations. The [candidate CSV](awards-audit/candidates.csv) is a flat review sheet containing all 109 additional candidates, including the 35 marked excluded; 59 marked applied and 15 deferred. Each applied candidate links to its public award ID. Neither is consumed by the public website.

## Items that should not become “best paper” claims

- NeurIPS spotlight/oral selections are presentation categories. In particular, the forecasting paper's verified publication year is 2024 despite a conflicting 2025 CV entry.
- Altmetric Top 100 and JMIR most-tweeted rankings measure attention; they are not paper prizes.
- Team awards must credit the team. The [CREATE accessibility award](https://www.washington.edu/accesstech/get-involved/events/awards/2024-digital-accessibility-awards/) should not appear as an individual Tim award; the Madrona prize belongs to the named Social Futures Lab team.
- Finalist/nominee entries (NCWIT, Schmidt, AAUW, IBM, dissertation nominations, Landolt) do not establish a win.
- A decorative “Geocities Spirit Award,” coauthors' neighboring CV entries, and awards attached to reposted papers were excluded.
- Same-name false matches were filtered, including the Duke Ethan Hsu, a Boston University Melih Yilmaz, other Cheng Li researchers, and Michael Merrill musicians/lawyers.
